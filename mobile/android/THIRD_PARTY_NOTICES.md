Native source is retrieved from pinned public repositories by
`mobile/ipad/scripts/prepare_dependencies.sh` and CMake. Application source,
Android adaptations, build scripts and pins are in this repository. libkiwix
source/build recipes are at https://github.com/kiwix/android-libkiwix and
https://github.com/kiwix/libkiwix; libzim is at https://github.com/openzim/libzim.
Use source releases matching the published 2.6.0 Android AAR.

The installer catalog is a data manifest copied from PR200; its SHA-256 is
`3346dee5d442c7f36e55cdbb87a1512ec7f76f1ab28924ca0dad2337764cb82c`.
It describes separately downloaded content/model weights. They do not become
MIT or GPL because this application reads them. Gemma terms and the licenses of
Wikipedia/Wikibooks/other archives apply to their respective files. No production
model or full library pack is embedded in the APK.

Test content generated by `tools/android/make_fixture.py` is synthetic.
Downloaded tiny-model/ZIM fixtures retain their upstream notices and are used
only by instrumentation, never included in the phone application.
